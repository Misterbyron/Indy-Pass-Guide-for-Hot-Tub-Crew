My tiered picks: drive-to hills first, then short flights, out west, and trip ideas for Europe and Japan. Ranked within each tier, with the lifts and après worth knowing about: 🍺 is good après, 🍺🍺 is great, 🛏️ means you can sleep slopeside, 🚆 means you can do it without a car, and ✨ marks extras like spas, onsen and the Northern Lights. Tap a resort for vertical, trip length and the website.

Season strategy: the East Coast season is short, so spend January and February on the drive-to hills. Save the late-season places, like Jay Peak, Loveland, the Alps and Lapland, for March and April.

Minimum stays: the two-hour hills are day trips. Greek Peak and Berkshire East are worth one night, since night skiing lets you ski the day you arrive. Magic and anything farther without night skiing needs two nights, and Middlebury by train needs three.

Long weekends: MLK (Jan 16–18) fits Middlebury by train with only a Friday afternoon off: up Friday, ski Saturday and Sunday, train home Monday morning. If the snow isn’t in yet, do Ragged + Waterville over three nights instead. Presidents’ Day (Feb 13–15) is peak snow and peak crowds, since it’s school vacation week, so use the extra night at Magic or in New Hampshire and book Indy reservations early. Take a Friday or Monday off in March for Jay Peak by car, about 6.5 hours each way, over three nights.
